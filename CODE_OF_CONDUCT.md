# Code of Conduct

GateMux is committed to a respectful, technically serious, and inclusive community.

## Expected Behavior

- be respectful and direct
- assume good intent
- focus on technical substance
- accept feedback without personal attacks
- help keep discussions useful for maintainers and contributors

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment or intimidation
- discriminatory language or behavior
- personal attacks
- doxxing or threats
- intentionally disruptive spam or bad-faith engagement

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or block participants whose behavior harms the project or its contributors.

## Reporting

If you encounter unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainers rather than escalating in public threads.
